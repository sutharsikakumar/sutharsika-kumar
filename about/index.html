I’m Sutharsika. I study Applied Math and Computer Science at UNC Chapel Hill, graduating in 2028 (expected Summa Cum Laude / Highest Distinction), with a minor in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics. Why not?

The bigger purpose is to do as much good for the world as possible and to challenge myself as much as possible along the way. For me, right now, that is research in pre-training and post-training. I fear I am quite the generalist, but I want to understand the whole system, and maybe in the future I will delve into one niche that I am particularly moved by.

Currently, I’m working on reinforcement learning policy and an autonomous robotic laboratory, advised by Dr. Haozhe Wang at Duke, building on Co-Scientist. Previously, I collaborated with researchers at Google DeepMind on ATOMIC, a zero-shot autonomous microscopy framework for 2D materials. See research for papers.

Away from research, I love drawing, reading books with my sister, and finding a new café. Anything orange-flavored in my coffee is a yes.
