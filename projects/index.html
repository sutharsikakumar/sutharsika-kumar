  • GRPO length bias (Python): A toy experiment on the length bias hiding in GRPO: two losses, the same accuracy, differently shaped mistakes. Write-up.
  • Raman Arena (Python): Benchmarks LLMs on Raman spectrum interpretation (denoise, peak extraction, material and layer classification), then ranks outputs head-to-head with an LLM judge in an Elo tournament.
  • parallax-long-context (Python): lctx-bench: an evaluation harness measuring how accuracy degrades with context length and information position for custom attention mechanisms, paired with the Parallax reference kernels.
  • llm-spectroscopy (Python): LLM benchmarking pipeline for domain-specific materials science spectra such as Raman and XRD, using data from the Wang Lab at Duke.
  • SUPERSemi: Self-evolving agent that rebuilds itself through feedback from a closed loop of semiconductor recipe generation and fabrication result synthesis.
  • algorithm-animations (manim): Animations of algorithms using manim.
  • im-here (TypeScript): A little cute friend that is on your friends’ screen.
  • tac-countdown (JavaScript): A countdown game.
