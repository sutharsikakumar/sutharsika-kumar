Current Interests

My goal is to help build human-aligned superintelligence in my lifetime, and to do work that moves science forward along the way. I want to understand how models learn and how to make them learn better: reinforcement learning, world models, and language models as policy optimizers (my GRPO write-up is an early look at this), plus pre-training, especially how attention design shapes long-context behavior.

Right now I work on reinforcement learning for atomic layer etching (a step in modern transistor fabrication), inside an autonomous robotic laboratory where agents run real experiments in closed loop, advised by Dr. Haozhe Wang at Duke. Before that, I worked with Google DeepMind on ATOMIC, a zero-shot autonomous microscopy framework for 2D materials. I also contribute to the open-source Axiom Stacks project.
